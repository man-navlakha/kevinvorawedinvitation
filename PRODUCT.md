# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Wedding guests opening a personal digital invitation, often on a phone, who need to immediately understand whose celebration this is, when and where it takes place, and then explore the couple's story and event details at an unhurried pace.

## Product Purpose

Create a memorable, emotionally resonant wedding invitation that combines the intimacy of a luxury printed card with cinematic photography and gentle interaction. Success means guests feel personally welcomed, can absorb the essential celebration details quickly, and enjoy discovering the full invitation.

## Brand Personality

Luminous, intimate, ceremonial. The experience should feel expensive, calm, romantic, and rooted in Indian wedding hospitality without becoming ornate or theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic wedding-template layouts, bright or over-saturated wedding palettes, excessive gradients, crowded ornament, novelty effects, large amounts of copy, repetitive cards, and aggressive animation. Do not copy the structure or styling of the original inspiration site.

## Design Principles

1. Let one decisive photograph carry the emotion of each major moment.
2. Reveal information with ceremony, but keep essential details instantly legible.
3. Use ornament like fine jewelry: sparse, intentional, and supportive of the couple's story.
4. Favor editorial pacing and generous space over interface chrome.
5. Make every interaction feel gentle, quick, and optional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast over photography, semantic headings and landmarks, useful alternative text, visible keyboard focus, touch-friendly targets, and a complete experience with motion reduced or disabled when requested by the operating system.
